# EncodeX for Mac

**EncodeX for Mac** brings the full power of **FFmpeg** to macOS behind a friendly interface — no command line required. It's free, open source, and runs 100% on your Mac.

## A Free FFmpeg GUI for macOS

Mac users pick EncodeX because it's designed around the Mac way of working:

- **Apple Silicon ready** — a native Apple Silicon build plus Intel support
- **VideoToolbox hardware acceleration** — fast encodes on M-series chips
- **Drag and drop** — no terminal knowledge needed
- **Batch folders** — process hundreds of files at once
- **Offline & private** — your videos never leave your Mac

## FAQ

**Does EncodeX work on Apple Silicon Macs?** Yes, there is a dedicated Apple Silicon build that runs natively and uses the VideoToolbox hardware encoder.

**Can I convert video to ProRes on a Mac with EncodeX?** Yes — EncodeX includes ready-made ProRes 422 and 4444 profiles, perfect for Final Cut and DaVinci Resolve workflows.

**Does EncodeX upload my videos?** No. All conversion happens locally on your Mac.

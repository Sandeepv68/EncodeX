# HandBrake Alternative

HandBrake is a legend — and a great reason to look at **EncodeX** as your next **HandBrake alternative**. EncodeX is a free, open-source **FFmpeg GUI** that takes the same encoding engine you know and puts it in a friendlier, more flexible package.

## What Is HandBrake?

HandBrake is a popular, free video transcoder — especially loved for disk ripping (DVD, Blu-ray). It's been around for years, and if you've ever encoded a video, there's a good chance you used it.

It's great software. But it's not for everyone — which is why a modern HandBrake alternative can win you over.

## Why Switch to EncodeX?

- **A gentler learning curve** — the GUI is task-based ("Convert", "Compress", "Cut"), not codec-based
- **More formats and codecs** — 51 video, 27 audio, 56 pixel formats
- **Better batch support** — up to 4 parallel jobs with pause/resume and drag-and-drop ordering
- **Built for macOS** — native Apple Silicon build with VideoToolbox
- **Active, friendly community** — we're open source and always improving

## Do I Really Need to Switch?

Not necessarily! HandBrake is excellent for disk ripping. But if you want a simpler UI, richer batch features, more formats, or one app that also compresses images and extracts audio — EncodeX is worth a download.

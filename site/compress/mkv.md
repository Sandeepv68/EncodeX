## Why Compress MKV Files?

MKV is a favorite container for high-quality video — which is also why it gets big:

- **Huge remuxes** — untouched Blu-ray rips can exceed tens of gigabytes
- **Archive space** — collections eat drives quickly
- **Sharing** — MKV files are too large for many chat and email apps
- **Streaming** — media servers perform better with reasonable bitrates

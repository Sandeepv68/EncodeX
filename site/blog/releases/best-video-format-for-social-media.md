# Best Video Format for YouTube & Social Media in 2026

Uploading to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or X and wondering which format to use? The "best" format balances **quality**, **compatibility**, and **upload speed**. This guide cuts through the jargon and gives you a simple, reliable answer.

## The Short Answer

Upload **MP4** with the **H.264** codec at 1080p for most platforms. It's the most compatible container/codec combo in the world — every platform accepts it, and you'll avoid re-encoding surprises.

## Containers vs. Codecs (In Plain English)

It's worth separating two things people often confuse:

- A **container** (like [MP4](/convert/mov-to-mp4) or [MKV](/convert/mkv-to-mp4)) is the "box" that holds the video and audio tracks.
- A **codec** (like [H.264](/codecs/h264), [H.265](/codecs/h265), or [AV1](/codecs/av1)) is how the video inside is compressed.

A modern high-quality choice is **MP4 with H.265/HEVC**, which gives roughly half the file size of H.264 at similar quality — great for uploads. If you want maximum compatibility, stick with H.264.

## Format by Platform

| Platform | Recommended format |
|----------|--------------------|
| YouTube | MP4 (H.264 or H.265), 1080p–4K |
| TikTok | MP4 (H.264), 1080x1920 vertical |
| Instagram Reels | MP4 (H.264), 1080x1920 vertical |
| X (Twitter) | MP4 (H.264), under 512 MB |
| Facebook | MP4 (H.264), 1080p |

## Should You Upload in 4K?

Uploading in 4K can help YouTube serve a better-quality encode to viewers, but only if your source genuinely benefits from it. For talking-head clips and most social content, **1080p is plenty** and uploads much faster.

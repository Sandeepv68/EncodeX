# How to Extract Audio from Video for Free

Want the sound from a video without the video? Maybe it's a lecture you want as an MP3 for your commute, an interview you'd like to edit as a podcast, or a concert recording you want on your phone. Extracting audio from video is easy with the right tool.

## What Does "Extracting Audio" Mean?

Extracting audio takes the sound track out of a video file and saves it as a standalone audio file — like **MP3**, **M4A**, **FLAC**, or **WAV** — with no video at all. This is great for:

- Turning **lectures and classes** into portable audio
- Turning **interviews** into editable podcast clips
- Making an **MP3** from a music video or concert
- **Archiving** just the sound to save storage space

## Which Audio Format Should You Pick?

| Format | Best for |
|--------|----------|
| **MP3** | Maximum compatibility with phones, cars, and players |
| **M4A / AAC** | Smaller files at good quality |
| **FLAC** | Lossless audio for critical listening |
| **WAV** | Uncompressed audio for editing |

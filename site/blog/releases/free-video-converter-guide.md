# How to Convert Video Formats for Free in 2026

Got a video that won't play on your phone? A file that's too big to email? Or maybe you just want the audio from a YouTube lecture? Converting between video formats is one of those tasks that sounds complicated but is actually simple — if you have the right tool.

## Why Videos Need Converting

Every device, app, and platform has its own preferred video format. Here are some common scenarios:

- **Phone recorded in MKV** but your TV only plays MP4
- **Editing software wants ProRes** but your footage is H.265
- **File is 4 GB** and you need to email a smaller version
- **You want just the audio** from a lecture or podcast video

The fix is always the same: convert the file to a format your target device or app understands.

## The Easy Way: Use EncodeX

[EncodeX](/) is a free, open-source desktop app that handles all of this. Here's how to convert a video:

### Step 1: Download and Install

Head to the [download page](/download) and grab the version for your operating system. Install it like any other app.

### Step 2: Open Your Video

Launch EncodeX and drag your video file onto the window, or click to browse for it.

### Step 3: Pick Your Format

Choose what you want to convert to. Not sure? Here are safe defaults:

| Goal | Format |
|------|--------|
| Play on any device | MP4 (H.264) |
| Smaller file size | MP4 (H.265) |
| Edit in most software | MOV or MP4 |
| Web upload | WebM or MP4 |
| Extract audio only | MP3 or FLAC |

### Step 4: Click Convert

That's it. EncodeX shows a progress bar and notifies you when it's done.

## Other Things You Can Do

### Extract Audio from Video

Drop in any video, choose MP3 as the output format, and you'll get a standalone audio file. Great for:

- Turning lecture recordings into listen-on-the-go audio
- Extracting songs from concert videos
- Saving podcast interviews as audio-only

### Trim Videos

Switch to the trim tab, drag the start and end markers to the section you want, and save. The built-in player shows thumbnails and sound waves so you can find the exact moment.

### Compress Images

Got photos that are too big to share? EncodeX shrinks them while showing a live preview so you can see the quality before saving.

### Batch Processing

Drag in a whole folder of files. EncodeX processes them one by one, showing progress for each. You can add files while it's running, pause, or reorder the queue.

## Why Not Use an Online Converter?

Online converters seem convenient, but they come with catches:

- **File size limits** — most cap you at 500 MB or less
- **Privacy risk** — your files are uploaded to someone else's server
- **Slow** — upload, wait, download adds minutes to a two-minute task
- **Watermarks** — many free tiers stamp your output

With EncodeX, everything happens on your computer. No upload, no waiting, no surprises.

# Free Audio Converter for Every Format

Need to turn a song into an MP3, or a lecture recording into a smaller file? **EncodeX** is a **free audio converter** that changes audio between dozens of formats — MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG and more — right on your computer, with no uploads and no watermarks.

## Convert Between Any Audio Format

- **MP3** — the universal format for phones, cars, and players
- **FLAC** — lossless, for people who care about perfect quality
- **WAV** — uncompressed, great for editing
- **M4A, AAC, OGG, Opus** and more

If you're not sure which to pick, EncodeX suggests a sensible default. **WAV to MP3**, **FLAC to MP3**, and **MP3 to FLAC** are one-click jobs.

## Turn Videos Into Music

An **audio converter** that also extracts audio: drop in any video and pull out the sound as an MP3 (or another format). Perfect for lectures, interviews, podcasts and concert recordings.

## Why Convert AVI to MKV?

AVI (Audio Video Interleave) dates back to the 1990s and stores video in an outdated, rigid way. **MKV (Matroska)** is a modern, flexible container that supports subtitles, multiple audio tracks, chapters, and better compression — a favorite for archiving and media servers.

Common reasons people **AVI to MKV**:

- You want **subtitles** and **multiple audio tracks** in one file
- You're **building a home library** for Plex, Jellyfin, or Kodi
- Your AVI files are too big — MKV can hold modern codecs that are much smaller
- You want to **archive video** in a flexible, future-proof format

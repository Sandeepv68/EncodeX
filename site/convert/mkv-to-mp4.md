## Why Convert MKV to MP4?

MKV (Matroska) is a wonderfully flexible format loved by video enthusiasts for subtitles, multiple audio tracks, and quality. But it has a drawback: **not every device and player supports MKV**. MP4, by contrast, plays on almost everything — phones, TVs, consoles, editing software, and browsers.

Common reasons people **MKV to MP4**:

- Your **TV, phone, or car stereo** won't play MKV
- You're **uploading to a website** that only accepts MP4
- You want to **edit** the video in software that doesn't open MKV
- You need the file to work in a **group chat** or email attachment

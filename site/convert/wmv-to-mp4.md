## Why Convert WMV to MP4?

WMV files often won't play on Macs, phones, tablets, TVs, or in modern browsers and editing apps. MP4 is the universal format that works almost everywhere, with **smaller files** and full **compatibility**.

Common reasons people **WMV to MP4**:

- You're **uploading to a website** that only accepts MP4
- You want to **edit** the video in software that doesn't read WMV

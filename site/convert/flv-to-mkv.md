## Why Convert FLV to MKV?

FLV files are increasingly hard to play: the Flash Player plugin is gone, and most modern devices, TVs, phones, editing apps, and browsers dropped support years ago. **MKV (Matroska)** gives you a modern, flexible container that supports subtitles, multiple audio tracks, and modern, much smaller codecs.

Common reasons people **FLV to MKV**:

- You found an **old Flash download** that no longer plays
- You want **subtitles** and **multiple audio tracks** in one file
- You're **building a home library** for Plex, Jellyfin, or Kodi
- You want a **smaller, modern file** to archive or keep

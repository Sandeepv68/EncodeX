## Why Convert M4V to MP4?

M4V files are frequently **DRM-protected** or encoded to only play on Apple hardware, which is a problem on Android phones, Windows PCs, TVs, and many apps. **MP4** is the open, universal format that plays everywhere.

Common reasons people **M4V to MP4**:

- Your **Android phone, Windows PC, or TV** won't play an M4V
- You bought or rented a movie on **iTunes** and want it portable
- You want to **edit** the video in software that doesn't read M4V
- You want a **universally compatible** MP4 to share

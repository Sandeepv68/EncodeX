# Convert MP4 to MKV

**MP4** is the most compatible format around, but **MKV (Matroska)** offers far more flexibility — subtitles, multiple audio tracks, chapter markers, and lossless quality. ## Why Convert MP4 to MKV?

Common reasons people go from **MP4 to MKV**:

- You want to **add subtitles** or multiple audio tracks without re-encoding
- You're **muxing content** for a media server (Plex, Jellyfin, Kodi) that loves MKV
- You want to **preserve lossless quality** while changing the container
- You need **chapter markers** for long videos
- You want to keep a **single file** for all audio languages

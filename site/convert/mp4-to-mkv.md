## Why Convert MP4 to MKV?

Common reasons people go from **MP4 to MKV**:

- You want to **add subtitles** or multiple audio tracks
- You're **muxing content** for a media server (Plex, Jellyfin, Kodi) that loves MKV
- You want to **keep the original streams** while changing the container
- You need **chapter markers** for long videos
- You want to keep a **single file** for all audio languages

**Does converting MP4 to MKV lose quality?** It depends on the mode. Stream copy keeps the video and audio identical when the codecs fit MKV. A default re-encode keeps quality high but is not identical to the source.

**Why would I want MKV instead of MP4?** MKV supports soft subtitles, unlimited audio tracks, chapters, and attachment files — which makes it a favorite for archiving and media servers.

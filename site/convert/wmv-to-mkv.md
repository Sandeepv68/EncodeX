# Convert WMV to MKV

**WMV** (Windows Media Video) is a Microsoft format that mostly stays on Windows. Convert your **WMV to MKV** in a few clicks with **EncodeX** — a free **WMV to MKV converter** that runs entirely on your computer.

## Why Convert WMV to MKV?

WMV files often won't play on Macs, phones, tablets, TVs, or in modern browsers and editing apps. **MKV (Matroska)** is a modern, flexible container that supports subtitles, multiple audio tracks, chapters, and better compression — a favorite for archiving and media servers.

Common reasons people **WMV to MKV**:

- You need the video to **play on a Mac, phone, or tablet**
- You want **subtitles** and **multiple audio tracks** in one file
- You're **building a home library** for Plex, Jellyfin, or Kodi
- You want a **smaller file** with a modern, flexible codec

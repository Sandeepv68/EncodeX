## Why Convert FLV to MP4?

FLV files are increasingly hard to play: the Flash Player plugin is gone, and most modern devices, TVs, phones, editing apps, and browsers dropped support years ago. **MP4 is the universal modern format** — it plays everywhere and is much smaller for the same quality.

Common reasons people **FLV to MP4**:

- You found an **old Flash download** that no longer plays
- Your **phone, TV, or tablet** won't open FLV files
- You want to **edit** the video in modern software
- You want a **smaller, more compatible** file to share

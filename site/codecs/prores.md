# ProRes Converter

**Apple ProRes** is the professional intermediate codec used across the film and broadcast industry — the format editors love to cut with. **EncodeX** is a free **ProRes converter** that lets you transcode to ProRes 422, ProRes 4444, and more on Windows, Mac, and Linux.

## Why ProRes?

ProRes is designed for **editing performance**, not delivery size:

- **Excellent quality** that edits smoothly even on modest hardware
- **Keyframe-friendly** — scrubbing and cutting feel instant
- **Industry standard** — Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and more
- **Great for archival masters** before final delivery

## ProRes Encoder with EncodeX

Encoding to **ProRes** with EncodeX is easy:

1. [Download EncodeX for free](/download)
2. Drag your video onto the window
3. Choose a **ProRes** profile (422, 422HQ, 4444, and more) and click Convert

> **Note:** ProRes files are large — it's an intermediate format for editing, not delivery. Use it when you're preparing a master for an editing session, then export a small delivery file afterwards.

## When to Use ProRes

- Choose **ProRes 422 / HQ** for broadcast-quality editing masters
- Choose **ProRes 4444** when you need an alpha channel or maximum fidelity
- Choose **H.264** for final delivery to browsers and devices
- Choose **AV1** for the smallest archival files

EncodeX ships a full set of ready-made ProRes profiles, so the right settings fill themselves in.

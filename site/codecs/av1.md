# AV1 Encoder & Video Converter

**AV1** is the most efficient video codec available today — giving you the **smallest file size for a given quality** of any mainstream codec. **EncodeX** is a free **AV1 video converter** that lets you convert to AV1 when you need maximum compression.

## Why AV1?

AV1 is the codec of the future, already used by YouTube, Netflix, and browsers:

- **Best compression** — up to ~30% smaller than H.265 at the same quality
- **Royalty-free** and open standard
- **Future-proof** — supported by modern browsers and newer devices
- **Ideal for archiving and web video** where size matters most

## When to Use AV1 vs H.265

- Choose **H.264** for maximum compatibility with older devices
- Choose **H.265** for a great balance of size and compatibility
- Choose **AV1** for the absolute smallest files and future-proofing

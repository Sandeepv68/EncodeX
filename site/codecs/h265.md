# H.265 / HEVC Encoder & Video Converter

**H.265 (HEVC)** is the modern successor to H.264, delivering **roughly half the file size for the same quality**. **EncodeX** is a free **H.265 video converter** that lets you convert any video to HEVC with a couple of clicks — perfect for shrinking your library.

## Why H.265 / HEVC?

H.265 is the **h265 encoder** that modern hardware supports natively:

- **Half the file size** of H.264 at similar quality
- **4K and HDR ready** — built for modern video
- **Widely supported** on phones, TVs, and consoles from the last several years
- **Hardware accelerated** — fast to encode on modern GPUs

This makes HEVC the ideal choice for **video compression** and shrinking **large files** while keeping great quality.

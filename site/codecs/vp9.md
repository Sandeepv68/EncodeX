# VP9 Encoder & Video Converter

**VP9** is Google's open, royalty-free video codec — the workhorse behind YouTube, Chrome, and Android. **EncodeX** is a free **VP9 video converter** that lets you encode to VP9 when you want efficient, open, web-ready video.

## Why VP9?

VP9 packs a lot of punch for a codec that's free to use:

- **Excellent compression** — roughly half the size of H.264 at similar quality
- **Royalty-free** and open standard
- **Web-ready** — supported by modern browsers, YouTube, and Android
- **Great for streaming** — efficient on high-res and high-refresh content

> **Note:** VP9 encoding is slower than H.264 because of its aggressive compression. It shines for web delivery, archiving, and saving space — not for quick one-off conversions on old hardware.

## When to Use VP9 vs AV1 vs H.264

- Choose **H.264** for maximum compatibility with older devices
- Choose **VP9** for an open, efficient web-ready codec
- Choose **AV1** for the absolute smallest files (VP9's successor)
- Choose **H.265/HEVC** for a great balance of size and compatibility

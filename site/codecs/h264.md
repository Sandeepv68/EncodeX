## Why H.264?

H.264 is the safe, universal choice:

- **Works everywhere** — phones, TVs, consoles, browsers, editing software
- **Efficient** — good quality with reasonable file sizes
- **Standard** — required by YouTube, Instagram, and most platforms
- **Fast to encode** — especially with hardware acceleration

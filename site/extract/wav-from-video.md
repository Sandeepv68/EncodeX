## WAV vs MP3: Which Should I Use?

- Choose **WAV** when you need lossless audio — for editing, mastering, or archiving
- Choose **MP3** when size matters more than fidelity — for phones, sharing, and casual listening

Use EncodeX to extract both: keep the WAV master, then convert it to MP3 when you're ready to share.

## FAQ

**Is WAV sound better than MP3?** WAV is uncompressed, so it's the true original audio — MP3 discards some data to save space. For editing and archiving, WAV is the safe choice.

**Does extracting WAV make huge files?** Yes — roughly 10 MB per minute of stereo 16-bit/48 kHz audio. That's the price of lossless quality.

**Can I batch-extract WAV from many videos?** Yes. EncodeX can extract audio from an entire folder of videos in one go.

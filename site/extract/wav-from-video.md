- Choose **WAV** when you need uncompressed audio — for editing, mastering, or archiving

## FAQ

**Is WAV sound better than MP3?** WAV is uncompressed, so it keeps the source audio as decoded, while MP3 discards some data to save space. If the source audio is already lossy (as most video audio is), WAV does not restore the lost detail. For editing and archiving, WAV is the safe choice.

**Does extracting WAV make huge files?** Yes — about 11.5 MB per minute of stereo 16-bit/48 kHz audio (48,000 × 2 channels × 2 bytes × 60 s). That's the price of uncompressed audio.

**Can I batch-extract WAV from many videos?** Yes. EncodeX can extract audio from an entire folder of videos in one go.
